TechNova Solutions Employee Handbook

Company Overview
TechNova Solutions is a software development company specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, and Web Development. The company was founded in 2021 and currently has over 250 employees working across multiple departments.

Working Hours
Standard office hours are Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Employees are expected to work 40 hours per week.
Flexible working hours are allowed between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM for start time, provided employees complete their required working hours.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Full-time remote work is allowed only for approved roles.
Employees must maintain a stable internet connection and attend all scheduled meetings.

Leave Policy
Every full-time employee receives:
- 20 paid annual leave days.
- 12 sick leave days.
- 10 public holidays.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward for up to 10 days into the next calendar year.

Performance Reviews
Performance evaluations are conducted twice each year.
Employees are evaluated based on:
- Technical skills
- Communication
- Team collaboration
- Problem-solving ability
- Project delivery

Training and Development
Every employee receives an annual learning budget of $1,500.
Employees are encouraged to complete certifications in cloud computing, AI, cybersecurity, and software development.

Laptop Policy
Every employee receives a company-issued laptop.
The laptop remains company property.
Employees must return the laptop on their final working day.

Expense Reimbursement
Employees may claim reimbursement for:
- Business travel
- Hotel accommodation
- Conference registration
- Client meetings
- Approved online courses

Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

Code of Conduct
Employees must:
- Respect colleagues.
- Maintain professionalism.
- Protect confidential company information.
- Follow all security policies.
- Avoid workplace harassment.

Information Security
Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory.
Employees must never share passwords.
Sensitive company data must not be stored on personal devices.

IT Support
IT support is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
Employees can contact support through the internal helpdesk portal or by emailing support@technova.com.

Project Management
The engineering department follows Agile Scrum.
Sprint duration is two weeks.
Daily stand-up meetings begin at 10:00 AM.

AI Development Guidelines
Developers should use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) whenever applications require access to company documents.
Embedding models convert text into numerical vectors that enable semantic search.
ChromaDB is the standard vector database used internally.
LangChain is used for building AI pipelines.
LangGraph is used for orchestrating AI workflows involving multiple steps and decision-making.

Customer Support
Support tickets are categorized as:
- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

Critical issues must receive an initial response within one hour.

Cloud Infrastructure
The company primarily uses Amazon Web Services (AWS).
Applications are deployed using Docker containers.
Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipelines are managed through GitHub Actions.

Development Standards
Python version 3.11 or newer should be used.
All projects should follow PEP 8 coding standards.
Unit tests must maintain at least 80% code coverage.

Database Standards
PostgreSQL is the primary production database.
Redis is used for caching.
ChromaDB stores vector embeddings for AI applications.
